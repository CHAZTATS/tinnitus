Important

This is an experimental tool based on published research. Consult a healthcare professional before use.

  • Start low and increase gradually. The sound should be comfortable, not loud.
  • Listen daily for approximately 60 minutes.
  • You can listen while doing other activities.
  • Use headphones for best results.
  • The study showed significant improvement after 6 weeks of daily listening.

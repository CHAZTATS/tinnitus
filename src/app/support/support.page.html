Common Questions

How do I start a session?

Open the app, set your frequency and volume, then tap Start Therapy.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Tinnitus Clinic is a wellness app and does not provide diagnosis or emergency care.

Can I use it in the background?

Yes. Therapy sessions are designed to continue during normal phone use.

 Privacy

The app stores your local therapy preferences on your device. If ads are enabled, third-party ad providers may process limited device data according to their policies.
